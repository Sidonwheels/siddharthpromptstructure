What Is a Prompt?

A prompt is the instruction you give an AI. Think of the AI as a fast, well-read assistant that knows nothing about your situation until you tell it.

Practice: run both prompts in any AI tool and compare the answers.

Siddharth Bhalla03 / 15
It predicts the most likely next words from patterns it has learned.
It only sees what you type, plus the conversation so far.
It can sound confident and still be wrong.

How Does AI Read Your Prompt?

You→Prompt→AI model→Output→You review

Clearer input leads to better output. The last step is the one most people skip: you are always the editor.

The Five-Step Framework

Every strong prompt follows the same simple path: Task, Context, References, Evaluate, Iterate.

Memory trick: Thoughtfully Create Really Excellent Inputs.

Siddharth Bhalla05 / 15
Persona: who the AI should act as
Action: a strong verb such as write, compare, plan
Format: table, list, or word count

T Is for Task

Say exactly what you want done. One clear job per prompt works best.

"You are a career coach. Write a LinkedIn headline for a fresher in data analytics. Give 5 options in a numbered list, under 12 words each."

Practice: turn "help with my resume" into a task with a persona and a format.

Siddharth Bhalla06 / 15
Audience: college freshers, busy managers
Goal: why you need this
Constraints: length, deadline, budget
Tone: warm, formal, playful

C Is for Context

Context is everything the AI cannot guess.

"Explain what an API is. Audience: first-year B.Com students with no tech background. Goal: a 2-minute workshop intro. Tone: friendly, with one everyday analogy."

Practice: ask for the same explanation for a child, then for a CEO.

Siddharth Bhalla07 / 15
Show one or two examples of what good looks like.
Examples can be text, a table, or a layout to copy.
Never paste private or confidential material into a public AI tool.

R Is for References

Show, don't just tell. Examples teach style faster than rules. This is called few-shot prompting.

"Here are two taglines I like: 1. 'Fuel your day, one sip at a time.' 2. 'Brewed slow. Enjoyed fast.' Write 3 new taglines for a mountain homestay in the same style."

E Is for Evaluate

AI can invent facts, numbers, and links. This is called a hallucination. Check every output against the six questions.

Practice: ask an AI for 3 statistics about remote work, then verify each one with a trusted source.

Siddharth Bhalla09 / 15
Too long? "Cut this to 100 words."
Too complex? "Explain it like I'm 15."
Too generic? "Add a real example from retail."
Need options? "Give 3 more in a different tone."
Not sure? "What would make this stronger?"

I Is for Iterate

Prompt→Output→Evaluate→Refine

Your first prompt is a draft. Keep the chat going and steer it, like giving feedback to a colleague.
